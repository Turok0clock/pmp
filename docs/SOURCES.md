## Distinctions that matter

- **PMP 2026:** People 33%, Process 41%, Business Environment 26%. The ECO defines coverage; the app does not infer an exam blueprint from a PMBOK edition alone.
- **PMBOK 8:** six principles and seven performance domains. The app uses this framing without presenting older edition structures as current.
- **Lifecycle versus focus areas:** course descriptions sometimes simplify these. The lesson explicitly distinguishes a context-specific lifecycle from recurring initiating, planning, executing, monitoring/controlling and closing work.
- **Course PMO categories:** supportive, controlling and directive are used as the course’s comparative categories; actual mandates may combine services and vary by organization.
- **Original scenarios:** answers are justified by the stated situation. They are not presented as official exam keys or copied Study Hall material.
- **Community heuristics:** “assess first,” “collaborate first” and “never escalate” are addressed as conditional or misleading shortcuts. Urgency, safety, evidence, obligations and decision authority govern the example response.
- **Learning methods:** retrieval, self-explanation, feedback, deliberate correction, direct work application, spacing and interleaving are app design choices. The review intervals are a transparent practical policy, not an official PMI rule.
- **Visual continuity:** FAR Academy was inspected in `Turok0clock/Security-Plus/far-academy/index.html`. Its board/progress pattern and physical-security examples informed this app. Its dark colors were intentionally replaced after the user asked for a light PMI-inspired palette.

Future modules should add source-specific references where technical depth increases. Verify substantive changes against primary PMI materials, especially when current course wording conflicts with the official framing. Do not use community posts as a substitute for PMI authority.
